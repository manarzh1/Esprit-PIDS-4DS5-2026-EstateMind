Price Estimation

Valuation intelligence

From a simple listing price to an explainable market estimate.

This module is designed to compare the asking price with local market behavior, property signals and short-term market momentum before presenting a valuation range.

XGBoost estimateLocal median benchmark90-day trend signal

Estimated fair range

A future property card can show whether the current price is fair, expensive or undervalued compared with similar listings.

Price confidence

The confidence level should depend on data depth, location coverage, property type and reliability of the source.

Market explanation

The goal is not only to predict a number, but to explain why the model believes that price is realistic.

1CollectUse price, area, type, city, source and trust score.
2BenchmarkCompare with local median and nearby market behavior.
3EstimateGenerate a fair price range instead of a single fragile number.
4ExplainShow the main reasons behind the valuation signal.

Risk before commitment

Evidence-based answer

The module should answer with references and a risk level instead of giving vague legal advice.
